Refactor Kodi player and window management in `resources/lib/player_bridge.py` into a derived projection of `SessionState` (implementing Rule R4 from `docs/STATE-TRANSFER-REFACTOR.md`), eliminating timer-driven repair polling in favor of version-triggered updates. Reconcile Kodi's playlist contents, track ordering, item titles, and active GUI windows (video fullscreen window `12005` vs. music visualization window `12006`) strictly through a unified `apply_projection(snapshot)` method that executes only when `snapshot.version` changes, ensuring ticks without state modifications perform zero mutations. Remove all periodic repair loops—specifically `_repair_fullscreen_windows`, `_ensure_music_window`, `_music_window_until`, `_repair_music_lane_start`, and title-patching routines such as `_patch_playlist_label`—or reduce any surviving checks strictly to log-only drift reporting, while ensuring native Kodi auto-advance events route through the reducer via `KodiAdvancedEvent` rather than patching the projection out of band.

- Implement Rule R4 as specified in `docs/STATE-TRANSFER-REFACTOR.md`: Kodi's playlist, windows, and titles must be rebuilt on `SessionState.version` changes, never polled for truth or repaired on a timer.

- Guarantee that a monitor tick with no change in snapshot `version` performs no state or UI mutation.
- Delete the timer repair loops `_repair_fullscreen_windows`, `_ensure_music_window`, `_music_window_until`, `_repair_music_lane_start`, and playlist title patching (`_patch_playlist_label`), or downgrade surviving checks to log-only drift reports.
- Ensure Kodi auto-advance is reported via `KodiAdvancedEvent` and committed through `StateOwner.apply()`, making the state owner the single source of truth that drives subsequent projection updates.
- Preserve existing stream management, `PlayGate` concurrency, and audio normalization pipelines (`resources/lib/audio_norm.py`).
